# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single user (the owner), in their own kitchen, using the site on a phone or tablet propped on the counter while making gelato. Hands are often busy or messy; attention is split between the screen and the work. The user glances at ingredient quantities and method steps mid-process rather than reading start to finish. It doubles as a personal showpiece: the owner takes pride in it and wants it to feel like a beautiful, premium object even though the audience is just themselves.

## Product Purpose

A personal gelato recipe app. The job: find a recipe fast, scale it between batch sizes (950 mL and 1.5 L), read quantities and steps clearly while cooking, and copy an ingredient list to a shopping note. Success is going from "what do I make / what's the amount" to the answer in seconds, with the app feeling like a crafted object the owner enjoys returning to.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, appetizing. Voice is a confident craftsperson: minimal labels, no chit-chat, no exclamation. The interface speaks through restraint and accuracy, the way a good gelateria lets the product speak.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered, emoji-everywhere UI where decoration competes with content
- Generic SaaS dashboard look (boxed cards, flat utility, no warmth)
- Busy multi-gradient surfaces and competing pastel badge colors
- Recipe-blog spam: giant hero photos, ad slots, life-story preambles, popups, "jump to recipe" friction

## Design Principles

1. **Glanceable in the kitchen** — the next thing the cook needs is always legible at arm's length, on a counter, at a glance.
2. **Quiet confidence** — restraint over decoration; the recipes are the content and the UI recedes.
3. **Beautiful through precision** — premium feel comes from spacing, type, and alignment done exactly right, not from ornament.
4. **Frictionless action** — scale, search, and copy happen in one tap; never make the cook hunt.
5. **Honest craft** — few high-quality elements done precisely, the way good gelato uses few excellent ingredients.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast minimum across text and interactive elements.
- Large, legible type sized for arm's-length reading on a counter.
- Generous tap targets for use with busy or messy hands.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; motion is enhancement, never required.
- Category and status signaling must not rely on color alone (pair with label, icon, or shape).
